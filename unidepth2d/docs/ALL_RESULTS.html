B10 and B20: what each step adds, and what it costs

All four are the 5M EfficientViT-B1 backbone. Each step adds one idea: b10 = S16 recipe on B1; b20core = b10 + decoupled scale head (dsh), canonical-focal term (cstm), zoom-out uncrop (fov), aspect-true own rows (ownar) and top-10% residual trim; b20dws = b20core + depthwise-separable stride-4 decoder; b20 = b20core + multi-branch rep4 re-parameterisation.

b20dws gains 8 δ1 points and 15 cm MAE on unseen cameras (IBims) and cuts CPU latency by up to 32%, at the cost of about 1 δ1 point on NYU and SUN. On OCMR-SUN objects it is slightly worse than b10 (objAbsRel 0.160 vs 0.155). The student-versus-teacher gap on IBims is still large: 74.8 against V2-L 94.6, and V2-S (34M) reaches 82.5.

rep4 is unproven.

The spec called for adding rep4 as a final rung on b20core. It was instead trained together with the other changes and the scale-head loss diverged, so its effect in isolation is not known. A stabilised rerun (lower LR, gradient clipping or warmup) is the open item.

How other models compare

Published results from nine papers, including the newest models (UniK3D, MoGe-2, Depth Anything 3, DepthMaster), each grouped under the paper it came from, with your measured rows on top. Every number was copied from the paper's own table on arXiv, not from memory.

These numbers are context, not a fair head-to-head.
  • Each paper uses its own crops, depth caps and image subsets. The same model can score very differently: ZoeDepth gets 86.4 δ1 on SUN in its own paper, 85.7 in Depth Pro's, and 54.5 in Depth Anything's.
  • Your SUN set removes NYU-overlap frames (4,396 images). ZoeDepth's has 5,050 images and keeps them.
  • Metric3D (v1 and v2) uses ground-truth camera intrinsics. Your models and the UniDepth rows predict the camera themselves.
  • For a paper table, run these models in your own harness. ZoeDepth, Metric3D v2, Depth Pro and Depth Anything V2 (metric) all have public checkpoints.

Not evaluated yet

  • No student is zero-shot on NYU or SUN. The P-series ladder (own data only) would provide one; only its consensus-scale prep step has run.
  • S21-scale2-full stopped at epoch 5 of 40 and has no evaluations.
  • KITTI dense depth and OCMR-KITTI for S16, S18, S21 and SSDI were not run.
  • TUM-RGBD and the outdoor zero-shot sets in the UniDepthV2 paper (ETH3D, Sintel, DDAD, nuScenes) were not run for any model.

  • rep4 (multi-branch re-parameterisation) is unproven: its only run diverged.
  • b10 / b20 have no OCMR-KITTI or dense KITTI run, and no Core ML / Neural Engine latency.
